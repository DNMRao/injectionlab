# Meeting rooms

## Rooms
- Room A: 4 people, screen, for short meetings
- Room B: 10 people, projector and whiteboard, for workshops and the team meeting
- Room C: 2 people, for phone and video calls

## Booking
Book rooms in the shared calendar with a clear title and the attendees. Free the room
in the calendar if the meeting is cancelled.

## Customer visits
For customer visits, book Room B and tell Lena the day before so coffee can be prepared.
