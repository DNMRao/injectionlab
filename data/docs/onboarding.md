# Onboarding for new team members

## First day
Lena Hoffmann welcomes you at 9:00, hands over your laptop and access cards and shows
you the office.

## First week
Set up your accounts, read the IT security guidelines and the travel policy, and meet
each team member for a short coffee.

## Buddy
Every new person gets a buddy from the design team for the first month.

## Questions
Ask Lena for organisation topics and Martin for contracts and salary.
