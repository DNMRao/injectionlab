# Project: Wagner Retail store redesign

## Goal
Redesign of the Wagner Retail flagship store in Hamburg: interior concept, signage and
a new logo. Customer contact: Julia Wagner (julia@wagner-retail.example).

## Timeline
Concept approved in September. Logo by Schmidt Design, final files due end of November.
Store opening planned for March 2027.
