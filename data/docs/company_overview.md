# Company overview: Nordlicht GmbH

## Who we are
Nordlicht GmbH is a small design agency in Hamburg, founded in 2019. We design shops,
brands and websites for retail customers. The team has 12 people.

## Team and roles
- Martin Klein, managing director and budget owner (martin@nordlicht.example)
- Lena Hoffmann, project manager and office lead (lena@nordlicht.example)
- Tom Becker, senior designer (tom@nordlicht.example)
- Other designers, a developer and a working student complete the team.

## Main customers
Our largest customer in 2026 is Wagner Retail (contact: Julia Wagner). Other customers
are local shops and two restaurant chains.
