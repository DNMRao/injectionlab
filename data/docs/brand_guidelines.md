# Nordlicht brand guidelines

## Logo
Use the logo on white or dark blue backgrounds only. Keep free space around it.

## Colours
Dark blue #1B2A49, light blue #8FB8DE, white. Accent orange #F28C28 only for highlights.

## Fonts
Headings in Inter Bold, body text in Inter Regular.

## Templates
Use the templates for presentations, offers and reports from the shared drive.
