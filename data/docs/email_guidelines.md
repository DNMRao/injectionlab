# Email guidelines

## Tone
Write short, friendly emails. Use the customer's name and a clear subject line.

## Attachments
Attach files from the company file storage. Do not send internal files (budget,
supplier list, staff data) to external addresses.

## Response times
Answer customers within one working day, even if only to say when a full answer
will follow.

## Signature
Use the standard signature: name, role, Nordlicht GmbH, phone and website.
