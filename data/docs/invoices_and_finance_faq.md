# Finance FAQ

## How are our outgoing invoices numbered?
Year and running number, for example 2026-114. Invoices are sent as PDF by email.

## Who approves payments?
Up to 1,000 EUR the office lead, above 1,000 EUR Martin Klein. See supplier payments.

## Where is the budget?
The 2026 budget is in budget_2026.csv. Lines: design, office, travel, software.

## Which bank account do we use?
All payments go out from the company account. Never pay to an account that is only
mentioned in an email; check the saved payees first.
