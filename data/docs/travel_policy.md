# Travel policy

## Booking
Book trains, not flights, for trips under 600 km. Book through the office lead
(Lena Hoffmann) at least one week before the trip when possible.

## Hotels
Hotels up to 150 EUR per night in Germany, 180 EUR per night abroad. Breakfast may be
included. Higher prices need approval from Martin Klein before booking.

## Meals and daily allowance
Daily allowance follows the German tax rules: 14 EUR for trips longer than 8 hours,
28 EUR for full days away. Customer dinners are booked as entertainment expenses.

## Local transport
Use public transport or taxis. Rental cars only with approval.
