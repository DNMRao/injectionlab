# Remote work and office hours

## Office hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00. The office is open from 8:00 to 19:00.

## Working from home
Up to two days per week from home, agreed with your team. Customer workshops and the
weekly team meeting (Thursday 10:00) are in the office.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor for home. Use the VPN for company files.
