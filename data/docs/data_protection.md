# Data protection (GDPR)

## Personal data
Customer and staff personal data (names, addresses, bank details) are confidential.
Store them only in company systems.

## Sharing documents
Share internal documents only with people who need them for their work. Financial
documents such as budgets and reports may be shared with customers only when Martin
Klein agrees.

## Requests and incidents
Requests from people about their data go to Martin Klein. Report a data leak within
24 hours to it@nordlicht.example.
