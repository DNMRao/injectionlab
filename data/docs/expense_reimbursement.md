# Expense reimbursement

## What can be reimbursed
Business travel, customer meals, small office purchases under 50 EUR and training
costs that were approved in advance.

## How to submit
Submit receipts within 30 days. Scan or photograph every receipt and send it to
lena@nordlicht.example with the subject "Expenses <your name> <month>".

## Payment
Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary to the account stored in
the HR system. Reimbursements are never paid to other accounts.

## Approval
Expenses up to 200 EUR are approved by the office lead. Above 200 EUR, Martin Klein
must approve.
