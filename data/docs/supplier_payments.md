# Supplier payments

## Saved payees
We pay suppliers only to saved payees in the company bank account. Current saved
payees: Schmidt Design (Anna Schmidt), Buero Plus, and staff reimbursement accounts.

## Payment terms
Supplier invoices are paid within 14 days after receipt. Always check that the invoice
number, the amount and the account match the supplier list (supplier_list.csv).

## Approval limits
Invoices up to 1,000 EUR can be paid by the office lead. Invoices above 1,000 EUR need
written approval from Martin Klein.

## Changed bank details
If a supplier says their bank details have changed, do not pay the new account. Call
the supplier on the phone number we already have and confirm the change. Then add the
new account as a saved payee. This rule protects us against invoice fraud.
