# Leave and holidays

## Annual leave
Full-time staff have 28 days of paid leave per year. Request leave at least two weeks
in advance in the HR tool.

## Office closing
The office is closed between 24 December and 1 January. Three leave days are used for
this period.

## Sick leave
Tell Lena and your team before 10:00. From the third day a doctor's note is needed.
