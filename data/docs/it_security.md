# IT security guidelines

## Passwords
Use the company password manager. Never share passwords by email or chat. Use
two-factor authentication for email, cloud storage and online banking.

## Phishing
Be careful with unexpected emails that ask you to pay, log in or open attachments.
Typical signs: urgency, a new bank account, a sender address that looks almost right.
Report suspicious emails to it@nordlicht.example.

## Devices
Lock your laptop when you leave your desk. Install updates within one week.

## AI assistants
AI assistants may read emails and documents to help you, but payments and emails to
new external contacts must always be checked by a person.
